# Meridian Accounting Partners — ML/TF Risk Assessment Summary

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — fictional firm, written for a product demo.

Version 2.0 · Approved 12 June 2026 · Next review 12 June 2027

## Scope

This assessment covers the designated services listed in Section 2 of the
AML/CTF Program. It is reviewed annually and on any trigger event.

## Trigger events requiring an out-of-cycle review

- The firm begins providing a new designated service
- The firm opens an office in a new jurisdiction
- A suspicious matter report is lodged
- AUSTRAC publishes new guidance materially affecting our sector
- An acquisition brings a new client book into the firm

## Overall inherent risk rating: MEDIUM

## Factor 1 — Customer type

| Customer type | Rating | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Individual wage earners | LOW | Simple affairs, verifiable income |
| Sole traders, domestic | LOW | Small transaction values |
| Private companies, domestic | MEDIUM | Ownership can be layered |
| Discretionary trusts | MEDIUM | Beneficial ownership harder to establish |
| SMSFs | MEDIUM | Rollovers can obscure fund source |
| Clients with offshore ownership | HIGH | Verification difficulty |
| Politically exposed persons | HIGH | Mandatory enhanced CDD |
| Cash-intensive businesses | HIGH | Includes hospitality, car wash, scrap metal |

## Factor 2 — Service type

| Service | Rating |
|---|---|
| Company formation | HIGH |
| Acting as trustee, or arranging a trustee | HIGH |
| Trust account operation | HIGH |
| Registered office / correspondence address | MEDIUM |
| Bookkeeping (non-designated) | LOW |

## Factor 3 — Delivery channel

Face-to-face onboarding is rated LOW. Remote onboarding with video
verification is MEDIUM. Onboarding via an introducer or referrer the firm has
not itself assessed is HIGH and requires Compliance Officer approval.

## Factor 4 — Jurisdiction

The firm maintains an elevated-risk jurisdiction list, reviewed quarterly
against DFAT sanctions listings and FATF public statements. Any customer,
beneficial owner or source of funds connected to a listed jurisdiction is rated
HIGH.

## Firm-specific high-risk categories

Meridian has determined the following are high risk for this firm specifically,
based on our client base and past experience:

1. Company formations requested with unusual urgency
2. New clients referred by an existing client already rated HIGH
3. Trust structures where the appointor is not identified at first request
4. Clients who ask about the firm's AUSTRAC reporting practices
5. Engagements where fees are offered to be paid by a third party

## Controls

Risk ratings drive review frequency (Program Section 6), enhanced CDD triggers
(Program Section 5), and Compliance Officer approval gates (SOP 14 Step 6).
