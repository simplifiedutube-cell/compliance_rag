# SOP 07 — Escalating unusual activity

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — fictional firm, written for a product demo.

Owner: AML/CTF Compliance Officer · Version 2.4 · Effective 1 July 2026

## Purpose

This procedure tells you what to do when something about a client or a
transaction does not look right. It applies to every member of staff.

## The rule

You are not required to decide whether something is suspicious. You are
required to report it internally so that someone else can decide.

If you are hesitating about whether something meets the threshold, that
hesitation is itself the trigger. Escalate.

## What to escalate

Escalate anything including, but not limited to:

- A client who is reluctant to provide identity documents or provides
  documents that do not match what they have told you
- Instructions to structure payments or invoices to stay below a threshold
- A request to route funds through the firm's trust account with no apparent
  commercial reason
- A client whose stated business activity does not match the transaction
  pattern you can see
- Any request to backdate a document
- Third-party funds arriving from a person unconnected to the engagement
- A client who asks whether the firm reports anything to AUSTRAC

## How to escalate

1. Complete the Unusual Activity Report form in Practice Manager. Take five
   minutes and write what you actually observed, not your conclusion.
2. Save the form to the client folder under /AML/UAR/.
3. Notify the Compliance Officer directly by internal message the same
   business day. Do not send it by email to a group address.
4. Continue to act normally toward the client.

## Escalation chain

Staff member → AML/CTF Compliance Officer (Practice Manager) → Managing
Partner → the board.

If the Compliance Officer is the subject of your concern, escalate directly to
the Managing Partner. If the Managing Partner is the subject, escalate to any
other equity partner.

## Tipping off — read this carefully

Once you have escalated, you must not disclose to the customer, or to anyone
outside the escalation chain, that:

- an internal report has been made,
- a suspicious matter report is being considered or has been lodged, or
- the firm is conducting any AML/CTF enquiry into them.

This includes hinting, delaying with an invented excuse that references the
enquiry, or telling a colleague not on the chain. If a client asks you
directly, say that you cannot discuss internal processes and refer them to the
Compliance Officer.

## Timeframe

The Compliance Officer must acknowledge an internal report within one business
day and record a decision within three business days.

## No detriment

No staff member has ever been, or will be, disadvantaged at Meridian for
escalating in good faith something that turned out to be innocent. Over-
reporting internally is the intended behaviour.
