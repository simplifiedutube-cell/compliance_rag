# Corvid Advisory — AML/CTF Program

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Corvid Advisory Pty Ltd is a fictional firm.
> This tenant exists to demonstrate data isolation between customers.

Version 1.3 · Approved 20 May 2026

## Section 1 — Purpose

This program sets out how Corvid Advisory Pty Ltd, a four-person corporate
advisory practice in Melbourne, meets its AML/CTF obligations.

Corvid deliberately operates a narrower service set than a general practice in
order to limit its designated-service footprint.

## Section 2 — Designated services provided

Corvid provides one category of designated service only: company formation and
registered office services.

Corvid does NOT operate a trust account, does not act as trustee, and does not
provide correspondence-address services. Staff must escalate immediately if a
client requests any of these — accepting such an engagement would change the
firm's obligations and requires board approval.

## Section 3 — Governance

The AML/CTF Compliance Officer is the Director of Operations. Given the firm's
size, the governing body is the two directors acting jointly.

## Section 4 — Customer due diligence

Corvid applies enhanced CDD to every customer regardless of risk rating. This
is a deliberate simplification: with a small client book, the cost of a single
standard is lower than the cost of maintaining a tiered process.

See CV-PROC-02.

## Section 5 — Reporting and record keeping

Corvid retains records for seven years and lodges all reports through the
Director of Operations.
