# SOP 11 — Record keeping and retention

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — fictional firm, written for a product demo.

Owner: AML/CTF Compliance Officer · Version 1.8 · Effective 1 July 2026

## Purpose

This procedure sets out what Meridian keeps, where, and for how long.

## What must be kept

- All customer identification and verification records, including copies of
  documents relied on and the screening results
- Records of the risk rating assigned and the reasoning
- All internal Unusual Activity Reports and the decisions made on them
- Copies of any report lodged with AUSTRAC and the lodgement receipt
- Records of the designated services provided to each customer
- Training records for every staff member
- Each version of the AML/CTF Program and each risk assessment

## Retention period

Meridian retains AML/CTF records for **seven years**.

For customer identification records the seven years runs from the date the
firm stops providing designated services to that customer, not from the date
the record was created. Staff commonly get this wrong — the clock starts at the
end of the relationship.

For transaction and report records the seven years runs from the date of the
transaction or report.

Meridian's retention period is set to meet the statutory minimum. Where the
Act or Rules specify a longer period for a particular record, the longer period
applies and the Compliance Officer will update this SOP.

## Where records are kept

Primary storage is Meridian Practice Manager, hosted in Australia. Nothing
AML-related is stored on local drives, personal devices, or in personal cloud
accounts.

Screening PDFs and identity document copies are stored in the client folder
under /AML/ with access restricted to partners and the Compliance Officer.

## Format

Electronic copies are acceptable provided they are legible and complete. Where
a document was sighted physically, the staff member records that they sighted
the original and scans the copy.

## Destruction

Records are not destroyed before the retention period expires. At expiry the
Compliance Officer approves destruction in writing and records what was
destroyed and when. Automated deletion is disabled on AML folders.

## Access requests

Any request from AUSTRAC, a regulator, or a law enforcement agency for records
is referred immediately to the Managing Partner. Staff do not respond directly
and do not tell the customer.
