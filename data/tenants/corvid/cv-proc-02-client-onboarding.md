# CV-PROC-02 — Client onboarding

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — fictional firm, for isolation testing.

Owner: Director of Operations · Version 1.1 · Effective 1 July 2026

## Principle

Corvid applies one onboarding standard to every client. There is no low-risk
pathway and no abbreviated process.

## Steps

1. Collect full name, date of birth and residential address for every
   individual, and ACN, registered office and directors for every company.
2. Verify against original or certified documents. Corvid does not accept
   electronic verification services.
3. Establish source of funds for the formation fee, in writing.
4. Screen all parties against sanctions and PEP lists.
5. Director of Operations approves every file personally before incorporation
   documents are lodged.

## Prohibited engagements

Corvid will not act where:

- the client will not identify the ultimate beneficial owner
- the incorporation is requested to complete within 24 hours
- fees are offered in cash
- the client requests nominee directors or nominee shareholders

## Escalation

All concerns go directly to the Director of Operations. Corvid has no
intermediate escalation tier.
