# Meridian Accounting Partners — AML/CTF Program

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Meridian Accounting Partners Pty Ltd is a
> fictional firm. This document was written for a product demonstration and is
> not compliance advice. It is not derived from any published template.

Version 2.1 · Approved by the governing body 12 June 2026 · Next review 12 June 2027

## Section 1 — Purpose and scope

This program sets out how Meridian Accounting Partners Pty Ltd (ABN 00 000 000 000,
"the firm") meets its obligations as a reporting entity under the Anti-Money
Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006.

The firm is a 12-person public practice in Sydney providing bookkeeping, tax
agent services, management accounting, company formation, and trust
administration services to approximately 340 clients.

This program applies to all partners, employees, contractors and labour-hire
staff of the firm without exception.

## Section 2 — Designated services provided

The firm has assessed its service lines and determined that the following are
designated services requiring compliance under the program:

- Company formation and registered office services
- Acting as, or arranging for another person to act as, a trustee of an express trust
- Managing client money through the firm's trust account
- Providing a business address or correspondence address for a client

The firm has determined the following are NOT designated services and are
recorded here so staff do not apply CDD unnecessarily: ordinary tax return
preparation, BAS lodgement, statutory audit, and management reporting.

Any new service line must be assessed against Schedule 1 before launch. The
AML/CTF Compliance Officer owns that assessment.

## Section 3 — Governance

The governing body is the partnership board, comprising the four equity partners.

The AML/CTF Compliance Officer is the Practice Manager. The role holder must be
of sufficient seniority to make decisions independently and has a direct
reporting line to the board without going through a partner.

The board receives a written AML/CTF report annually and on any material
incident. The Compliance Officer may convene the board at any time.

## Section 4 — ML/TF risk assessment

The firm maintains a written risk assessment reviewed annually and on any
trigger event. See the separate Risk Assessment Summary document.

Risk is assessed across four factors: customer type, service type, delivery
channel, and jurisdiction.

## Section 5 — Customer due diligence

Initial CDD is completed before a designated service is provided. There are no
exceptions to this rule at Meridian; staff must not begin work on a designated
service on the basis that CDD is "in progress".

Detailed procedures are in SOP 03 (individuals), SOP 14 (trusts), and SOP 04
(companies and partnerships).

Enhanced CDD applies to any customer rated HIGH, any politically exposed
person, and any customer with a beneficial owner resident in a jurisdiction on
the firm's elevated-risk list.

## Section 6 — Ongoing customer due diligence and transaction monitoring

Client files are reviewed at each engagement renewal and at minimum every
24 months for standard-risk clients, 12 months for medium, and 6 months for
high-risk clients.

Staff must report unusual activity through SOP 07 regardless of whether they
believe it will amount to a suspicious matter. The determination is not theirs
to make.

## Section 7 — Reporting

The Compliance Officer is the only person authorised to lodge a suspicious
matter report or threshold transaction report on behalf of the firm.

Staff must never discuss a suspicious matter report, or the fact that one is
being considered, with the customer or with anyone outside the escalation
chain. See SOP 07 for the tipping-off rules.

## Section 8 — Record keeping

Records are retained in accordance with SOP 11.

## Section 9 — Personnel due diligence and training

All staff complete AML/CTF induction training before being given file access,
and refresher training annually. Training completion is tracked by the Practice
Manager in the HR system.

Personnel due diligence is conducted at hire and on promotion into any role
with client-money authority. This comprises identity verification, a national
police check, and reference checks with two prior employers.

## Section 10 — Independent evaluation

The firm will commission an independent evaluation of this program no later
than 30 June 2029, and thereafter at intervals of no more than three years.
The evaluator must not have been involved in designing or operating the
program.
