# CV-PROC-05 — Records and retention

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — fictional firm, for isolation testing.

Owner: Director of Operations · Version 1.0

## Retention

Corvid retains all AML/CTF records for seven years from the end of the client
relationship.

## Storage

All records are held in the Corvid document management system, hosted in
Australia. Corvid does not permit any AML record to be stored in email.

## Annual attestation

Each director attests annually in writing that they have not stored client
identity material outside the document management system.
