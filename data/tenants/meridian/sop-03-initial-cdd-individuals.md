# SOP 03 — Initial customer due diligence: individual clients

> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — fictional firm, written for a product demo.

Owner: AML/CTF Compliance Officer · Version 3.0 · Effective 1 July 2026

## Purpose

This procedure sets out how Meridian staff collect and verify identity
information for individual customers and sole traders before providing a
designated service.

## When this applies

Apply this SOP whenever an individual or sole trader engages the firm for any
service listed in Section 2 of the AML/CTF Program. If you are unsure whether
the engagement involves a designated service, stop and ask the Compliance
Officer. Do not proceed on assumption.

## Step 1 — Collect

Collect the following before any work begins:

- Full name as it appears on the identity document
- Date of birth
- Residential address (not a PO box, not the firm's own address)
- If a sole trader: business name and ABN

Record these in the client record in Meridian Practice Manager under the AML
tab. Do not record them in file notes or email.

## Step 2 — Verify

Verify name and date of birth, and separately verify name and residential
address, using reliable and independent documentation.

Acceptable at Meridian:

- Australian passport, or foreign passport with a translated certification
- Australian driver licence (both sides)
- Medicare card plus one secondary document
- For address: a utility bill or bank statement dated within the last 3 months

Meridian does not accept: expired documents, photographs of documents sent by
SMS, or documents provided by a third party who is not the customer.

## Step 3 — Screen

Run the client name through the sanctions and PEP screening tool before
completing onboarding. Save the screening result PDF to the client folder even
where there is no match — a nil result is evidence that screening occurred.

## Step 4 — Risk rate

Assign a risk rating of LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH using the matrix in the Risk
Assessment Summary. Where you are between two ratings, take the higher.

Any HIGH rating triggers enhanced CDD and must be approved by the Compliance
Officer before the engagement proceeds.

## Step 5 — Record and complete

Complete the CDD checklist in Practice Manager and mark the client as
AML-cleared. The system will not permit a designated service to be billed
against a client who is not AML-cleared. This control is deliberate and must
not be overridden.

## Timeframes

Initial CDD must be completed before the designated service is provided.
Meridian does not operate any delayed-verification arrangement.

## If you cannot verify

If the customer cannot or will not provide acceptable documentation, do not
proceed and do not tell the customer that this may be reportable. Refer to the
Compliance Officer under SOP 07.
